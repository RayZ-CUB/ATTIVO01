# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tennis players of all skill levels (Beginner through Competitive) and verified tennis coaches. Users are active, outdoorsy, and community-oriented — typically checking the app before or after sessions, on the go, often in bright outdoor light. Coaches are professionals who need to manage their reputation and incoming session requests efficiently. Both groups value authenticity and local connection over corporate polish.

## Product Purpose

ATTIVO connects tennis players with verified coaches and brings local tennis communities together through a social feed and events calendar. It removes the friction of finding the right coach and makes local tennis culture visible and participatory. Success looks like a player booking their first session with a matched coach, and a community where local events feel alive and worth joining.

## Brand Personality

Community, athletic, premium. The tone is confident and direct — like a knowledgeable training partner, not a corporate platform. Warm but not soft. Energetic but not loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic fitness apps (Strava, Nike Run Club aesthetic — data-heavy, cold, metric-obsessed)
- Corporate SaaS look (navy + white, enterprise grids, "streamline your workflow" copy)
- AI-generated slop: cream/sand backgrounds, purple gradients, glassmorphism cards, identical icon-grid layouts, tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section
- Overcrowded dashboards with too many stats competing for attention

## Design Principles

1. **Accessibility first, beauty second** — color contrast, touch targets, and readable type are non-negotiable. The dark theme must be legible, not just atmospheric.
2. **Community over metrics** — surfaces people and events, not numbers and streaks. The feed and coach cards should feel human.
3. **Intentional color** — lime green `#c8f135` is the single accent. It earns its place on CTAs, active states, and focus rings. Everything else is dark neutrals.
4. **Motion with purpose** — animations guide attention and confirm actions. No decorative fades, no bounce easing, no spring overshoot.
5. **Distinctive, not generic** — every screen should feel unmistakably ATTIVO. Bebas Neue headings, DM Sans body, dark-on-dark card surfaces with lime accents are the signature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text must hit ≥4.5:1 contrast against card/panel backgrounds. All interactive elements need minimum 44×44pt touch targets. Reduced motion support required on all animations. The dark theme must not rely on color alone to convey state — use shape, weight, and label changes alongside color.
